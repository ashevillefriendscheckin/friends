First, put Friends Check-In on your home screen. It takes about a minute, and you only do it once.

On an iPhone

  1. Make sure this page is open in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, a square with an arrow pointing up. If you do not see it, tap the three dots first.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen." You may need to tap "More" first.
  4. Tap "Add."

On an Android phone

  1. Make sure this page is open in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen." If it asks, choose "Create shortcut."
  4. Tap "Add." If it asks again, tap "Add to home screen."

The icon may land on a later page of your home screen. Swipe left to find it, then press and hold it to move it.

From then on, tap the yellow icon to check in.
